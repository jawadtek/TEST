For the dough: 3 cups flour, 1 tbsp dry yeast, 1 tbsp sugar, 3 tbsp olive oil, a pinch of salt, and warm water.

For the sauce: 3 crushed tomatoes, 1 garlic clove, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp tomato paste, oregano, salt, and black pepper.

For toppings: Shredded mozzarella cheese, black olives, and green peppers or minced meat as desired.
