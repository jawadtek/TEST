For the dough: 3 cups flour, 1 tbsp dry yeast, 1 tbsp sugar, 3 tbsp olive oil, a pinch of salt, and warm water.

For the sauce: 3 crushed tomatoes, 1 garlic clove, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp tomato paste, oregano, salt, and black pepper.

For toppings: Shredded mozzarella cheese, black olives, and green peppers or minced meat as desired.

Step 1: Preparing the Sauce

Sauté the garlic in olive oil over low heat, add the tomatoes, tomato paste, and spices. Simmer until thickened, then let it cool down.

Step 2: Preparing and Kneading the Dough

In a bowl, mix flour, yeast, sugar, and salt. Add olive oil and warm water gradually, kneading for 5 to 8 minutes until soft and smooth.

Step 3: Fermentation (Rising)

Cover the dough with a cloth or plastic wrap and leave it in a warm place for 45 to 60 minutes until it doubles in size.

Step 4: Assembling the Pizza

Roll out the dough on a greased baking tray, spread the cooled tomato sauce evenly, and add the shredded cheese, olives, and toppings.

Step 5: Baking and Serving

Bake in a preheated oven at 200°C (400°F) for 12 to 15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted. Slice and serve hot!
